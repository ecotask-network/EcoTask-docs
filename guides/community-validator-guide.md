# Community Validator Guide

Community validators play a crucial role in EcoTask by verifying that tasks have been completed correctly.

## Becoming a Validator
1. Build a reputation by completing tasks successfully
2. Apply for validator status through the platform
3. Undergo a brief training on verification standards

## Verification Process
When a user submits proof:
1. **Auto-checks** run first (GPS zone, photo analysis, expiry check)
2. **Validators** review the proof and either approve or reject

## Review Criteria
### Approved
- Photo clearly shows the task being completed
- GPS coordinates match the task area
- Photo timestamp is within the task's active period

### Rejected
- Photo is blurry or unrelated
- GPS location is outside the task zone
- Evidence of duplicate submission
- Photo appears to be stock imagery or AI-generated

## Validator Rewards
Validators earn a small fee from each proof they verify. The exact amount depends on task complexity and validator tier.

## Ethics
- Be fair and consistent in your reviews
- Do not collude with submitters
- Report suspicious activity to platform admins
